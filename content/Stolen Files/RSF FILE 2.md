***RSF STAR SYSTEM MONITORING REPORT*** 
SUBJECT STAR SYSTEM: LEPHOR
PLANETS: VAEA, TITHOS-2, ARCADIA, X44 

SATELLITE RINGS 56, 57, 89
REPORTING AGENT: ASHI "THE PERIGRINE" MASHIHIRO 

"Annual reports of Lephor have become necessary due to the threat posed by the current raging arcadian civil war. What started on one Arcadian island as a fight for stronger resource control spiralled quickly into a planetary conflict worryingly fast. I have reason to believe AS119-SAKURA class ships previously used for trading and planetary defence have been retrofitted with space to ground weapons used primarily by NEW EXERGIS. Arcadia remains *highly* volatile due to this conflict and as a result is still classed by RSF as an X-CLASS "do not approach" world. The smaller satellite rings 89 and 57 report conflict being waged across the surfaces of Tithos-2 and X44. My guess is that Government Loyalists are attempting to make a resource grab and/or reconnect to arcadian colonists abandoned during the CRT's resurgence attacks on Lephor. should either faction succeed in accomplishing either of these tasks it could completely change the war, we must be careful that this cancer does not spread from lephor and the arcadians squabble is contained. Tithos-2 and X44 have vast resources that could allow the arcadians to wage this war across more systems. We alone have the power to stop this and must exercise our strength, these worlds **CANNOT** fall into the hands of a successful arcadian force" 

"Vaea remains stable, Convergence forces on its surface remain dormant since contact with the unknown Void based entity found there. will continue to monitor diligently" 

-Peregrine
