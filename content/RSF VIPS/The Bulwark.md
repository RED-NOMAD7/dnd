The Bulwark had an unusually normal career progression for the RSF, joined voluntarily as a footsoldier, slowly worked his way up the chain until the he got the position he wanted. He is highly skilled and while the least remarkable RSF commander in combat, is an absolutely terrifying strategic mastermind. a chess master from a young age, he has helped the RSF plan many attacks, whether justifiable or not. He has a friendly relationship with The Ward, and is extremely protective in a "daughter i never had" way. Calm and collected during battle, the ward is not someone to underestimate...
