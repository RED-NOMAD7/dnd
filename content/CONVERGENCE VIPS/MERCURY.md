**Mercury, the Convergence God Machine**

*Huge Construct, Directs the flow*

**Armor Class:** 22 (Divine Plating)  
**Hit Points:** 400 (30d12+180)  
**Speed:** 40 ft., Fly 80 ft. (hover)  
**Languages:** Convergence Binary, telepathic link to Convergence units  



---

### Legendary Actions:

**Radiant Wing Slash (Costs 1 Action):**  
Mercury makes a melee wing attack against one creature within 15 feet. *+15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.* *Hit:* 32 (4d12+6) radiant damage. Its red neon wings slash through the air with deadly precision, leaving trails of scorching light.

**Graceful Laceration (Costs 2 Actions):**  
Mercury spreads its wings, dealing 20 (4d8) radiant damage to all creatures within 30 feet and forcing each to make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is blinded until the end of its next turn.

**Overpower (Costs 3 Actions):**  
Mercury emits an overwhelming pulse of energy in a 60-foot radius. All hostile creatures must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of their next turn. Convergence units are instead healed for 20 (4d10) hit points.

---

### Description:

Mercury, one of the Convergence’s nine legendary *god machines*, commands an aura of terror and elegance as it hovers above the battlefield. Its form is slim and angular, covered in segmented white armor with black struts, and its wings are a striking display of red neon energy, extending from its back like blades of light. The wings pulse rhythmically, providing both grace and lethality, capable of slicing through enemies in moments.

Despite its threatening presence, Mercury moves with an eerie serenity, calculating each motion to devastate its enemies while preserving its own strength. As it flies, its glowing wings trace radiant arcs in the air, embodying a transcendent power that inspires awe and dread in those who witness it.

As the weakest of the 9 Convergence Gods that serve the Imperative, Mercury is an integral part of their mission to bring the universe to its knees. Despite this, its military applications *can not* be ignored. If every unit in the convergence is a part in a greater machine, then the God Machines are the core processors.
